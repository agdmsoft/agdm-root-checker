Uncommon Tools for an App in This Category

Optional, manual, opt-in checks — nothing runs in the background, nothing leaves your device.

NEW

📶 Sudden Network-Downgrade Indicator

Compares your current connection to your last manual check and flags it when the network suddenly drops (e.g. LTE to 2G) in a short window. Not a fake base-station detector — that needs low-level access no regular app can get on Android — just a "worth a second look" signal.

NEW

🌡️ Idle Heat Check

One tap compares your battery temperature against fixed thresholds and your own device's usual idle temperature — helpful context, not a diagnosis.

NEW

🔒 Camera & Microphone Privacy

See which installed apps currently hold camera/microphone permission, plus a direct link to Android's own Privacy Dashboard for the real usage history. Also included: an honest, research-backed breakdown of the "ads right after a conversation" myth.

NEW

📊 App Traffic

See how much Wi-Fi and mobile data other installed apps have used, broken down by app and by day/week/month. Just data-volume totals per app — never content, addresses, or browsing history.

What People Use It For

Illustrative examples of how the toolkit gets used — not customer quotes. AGDM Root Checker reports what it can actually measure; it doesn't identify who is responsible for anything it flags.
